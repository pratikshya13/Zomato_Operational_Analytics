# Zomato_Operational_Analytics
Zomato Operational Analytics &amp; Restaurant Expansion System | Advanced Excel, Data Modeling
Executed end-to-end data cleaning and preprocessing on a multi-country dataset of 9,000+ restaurants in Excel, resolving missing cuisine values via IF/VLOOKUP logic and standardizing multi-currency pricing to INR.
Analyzed operational metrics across 15 countries to identify high-potential expansion markets (Sri Lanka, Australia, Canada, Singapore, Brazil) characterized by low market saturation and strong customer ratings (3.5+).
Engineered multi-criteria array formulas and dynamic conditional formatting to pinpoint optimal pricing tiers (Ranges 1 & 4), voter engagement trends, and service gaps in online delivery and table booking systems.
Formulated data-backed expansion strategies covering target cities, cuisine alignment, and delivery service integration to maximize operational ROI.
